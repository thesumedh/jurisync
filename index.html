Problem Statement

Research Topic: Development of an MCP-based Cross-Jurisdictional Compliance Orchestration Layer for Dynamic Multi-Country Regulatory Guidance during AI-Assisted Application Development.

As AI coding assistants use the Model Context Protocol (MCP) to call tools and external data, applications are often deployed across multiple countries with diverging legal rules (binding legislation like EU AI Act, GDPR, India's DPDP Act vs voluntary frameworks like NIST AI RMF). JurisMCP proves that dynamic, development-time regulatory orchestration can catch architectural violations early—before costly redesigns or regulatory sanctions.
